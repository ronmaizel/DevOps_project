K8S architecture would be scalable, self-healing and redundant.
High availability is achieved by replicating web, app, and database containers. Patroni handles DB leader failover automatically.
Of course, if the master node fails, all services will jump to one of the worker nodes, meaning also node high availability is achieved.

The flow is exactly the same, only now every service has a DR service if it happens to fail.
We would have deployments for every service: A web deployment, an app deployment, and a Patroni deployment. There will also be 2
services: A LoadBalancer type service, and an internal app service.

IN DEPTH DEPLOYMENT AND SERVICE EXPLANATION:
The ingress controller deployemnt would consist of 2 replicas, fronted by a LoadBalacer type service. If one ingress pod fails,
traffic is routed to another pod. The external load balancer handles balancing traffic to the healthy ingress pods.
The web deployment will consist of 2 web replicas, traffic from the ingress is routed via a ClusterIP service to any available web pod.
The app deployemnt will consist of 2 app replicas. Requests incoming from the web deployment will be balanced between both pods,
and failed pods are restarted automatically by Kubernetes.
The Patroni deployment will consist of 3 replicas - a primary and 2 slaves. Patroni monitors the pods' health and promotes a slave
if the primary fails.
